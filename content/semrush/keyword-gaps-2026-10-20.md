# Keyword gaps vs. top-traffic clusters (Semrush, US, 2026-10-20)

Semrush has no organic data for thehonestreviewers.com yet, so a domain-vs-competitor
gap report isn't possible. Instead: broad-match keyword pulls for the 16 clusters that
bring the most traffic, checked against every slug in `src/pages/`. Everything below
has **no existing page**. Volume = US monthly searches, KD = Semrush keyword difficulty.

Service-intent terms ("near me", "contractors", "installation") and store terms
("home depot", "lowes") were left out — a review site can't win those.
